# IT Onboarding Guide — Northwind Outfitters

> NOTE: This is synthetic sample data written for a software demonstration.
> Northwind Outfitters is a fictional company. Nothing here is real.

## Getting your laptop

New hires receive a laptop on their first day. If your equipment has not
arrived, contact the IT help desk at helpdesk@northwind.example or extension
4357. Loaner laptops are available for urgent needs.

## VPN and remote access

To work remotely, install the company VPN client from the software portal and
sign in with your employee credentials plus multi-factor authentication. The
VPN is required to reach internal systems such as the inventory database and
the file server.

## Password policy

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and include uppercase letters,
lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols. Passwords expire every 90 days and
the last 5 passwords cannot be reused. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is
required for email, VPN, and the HR portal.

## Requesting software

To request new software, submit a ticket to helpdesk@northwind.example with
the software name, business justification, and your manager's approval.
Licensed software is typically provisioned within two business days. Free and
open-source tools still require a security review before installation.

## Reporting security incidents

If you suspect a phishing email or a compromised account, forward the message
to security@northwind.example and do not click any links. The security team
responds to incident reports within 4 business hours.
