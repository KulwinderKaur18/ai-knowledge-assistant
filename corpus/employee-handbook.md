# Employee Handbook — Northwind Outfitters

> NOTE: This is synthetic sample data written for a software demonstration.
> Northwind Outfitters is a fictional company. Nothing here is real.

## Working hours

Our standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM Atlantic time.
Employees are expected to be available during core collaboration hours from
10:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Overtime must be approved in advance by your manager and
is compensated at time-and-a-half for hourly employees.

## Remote work policy

Employees may work remotely up to two days per week after completing their
three-month probation period. Remote days must be agreed with your manager in
advance, and you must remain reachable on chat during working hours. Roles that
require on-site presence, such as warehouse staff, are not eligible.

## Statutory holidays

The company observes the following paid holidays: New Year's Day, Family Day,
Good Friday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, New Brunswick Day, Labour Day,
Thanksgiving, Remembrance Day, Christmas Day, and Boxing Day. If a holiday
falls on a weekend, the nearest weekday is observed instead.

## Dress code

Our dress code is casual. Employees should dress comfortably but presentably.
Safety footwear and high-visibility vests are required in warehouse areas at
all times.
