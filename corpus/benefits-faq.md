# Benefits & Payroll FAQ — Northwind Outfitters

> NOTE: This is synthetic sample data written for a software demonstration.
> Northwind Outfitters is a fictional company. Nothing here is real.

## Health and dental insurance

Health and dental coverage begins after three months of continuous employment.
The company pays 80% of the premium and the employee pays 20% through payroll
deduction. The plan covers prescription drugs, vision care up to $250 per
year, and basic dental services.

## Vacation

New employees receive 15 vacation days per year. You earn one additional day
per year of service, up to a maximum of 25 days. Vacation must be requested at
least two weeks in advance and approved by your manager. Up to 5 unused days
may be carried over to the following year.

## Parental leave

Employees are entitled to 12 weeks of parental leave following the birth or
adoption of a child. The company tops up government benefits to 80% of salary
for the first 8 weeks of leave.

## RRSP matching

The company matches employee RRSP contributions at 50 cents on the
dollar, up to 3% of your annual salary. You become fully vested in the
employer contributions after two years of service.

## Pay schedule

Payroll is processed bi-weekly, with pay deposited on alternating Fridays.
Pay stubs are available in the employee self-service portal.
