# Office Facilities & Safety — Northwind Outfitters

> NOTE: This is synthetic sample data written for a software demonstration.
> Northwind Outfitters is a fictional company. Nothing here is real.

## Emergency procedures

In case of fire, use the stairwells — never the elevators — and gather at the
muster point in the north parking lot. Emergency exit maps are posted beside
every elevator and in the break room. Call 911 first, then notify building
security at extension 4911.

## Fire drills

Fire drills are held on the first Tuesday of every month at 10:00 AM. All
employees must participate and proceed to the muster point. Floor wardens wear
orange vests and will guide you.

## First aid

First aid kits are located in the break room, at the reception desk, and in
the warehouse office. The designated first aiders are listed on the safety
bulletin board near the elevators.

## Reporting incidents

All workplace injuries and near-misses must be reported to your manager and to
facilities@northwind.example within 24 hours. An incident report form is
available on the company intranet under Health & Safety.

## Building access

Office hours for building access are 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM on weekdays. After
hours, use your key fob at the main entrance. Do not prop open secured doors
or share your fob with visitors — sign visitors in at reception instead.
